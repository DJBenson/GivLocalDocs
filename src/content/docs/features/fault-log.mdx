The Fault Log in the Inverter screen shows active and historical faults reported by your inverter.

## Viewing faults

Open **Inverter → Fault Log** to see a list of faults. Each entry shows the fault code and description. Active faults are shown at the top.

</a>

## Fault alert banners

When a fault is detected, GivLocal can display an alert banner on the Dashboard. Enable this in **Settings → General → Show Fault Codes**.

<a href={dashboardBanner.src} target="_blank" rel="noopener">
  <Image src={dashboardBanner} alt="Dashboard showing a fault alert banner listing the detected faults" />
</a>

## Clearing alerts

Once you have reviewed a fault you can clear the alert from within the Fault Log. This dismisses the banner but does not resolve the underlying fault on the inverter.

<Aside type="caution">
  Clearing an alert in GivLocal does not reset or acknowledge the fault on the inverter itself. If a fault persists, contact your installer or [Predictive Support LTD](https://predsupport.com/).
</Aside>
