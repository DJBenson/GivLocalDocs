title: Battery Diagnostics
description: Settings for calculated battery health data.
---

Open **Settings → Battery Diagnostics** to control display of calculated battery health data.

## Show Calculated State of Health

Displays a calculated battery health percentage on the battery cell detail sheet, when BMS capacity data is available from the inverter. See [Battery Cell Details](/features/battery-cell-details/) for more.
