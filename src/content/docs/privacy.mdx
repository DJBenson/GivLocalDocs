## Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the GivLocal mobile application and any
related services operated by Thomserve Business Analysis Solutions LTD (the
"Service Provider").

### What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application does not collect, log, or store personal information when you
download and use it. Registration is not required. GivLocal communicates
directly with your GivEnergy hardware over your own local network (or, if you
choose to set it up, over Tailscale or a port-forwarded connection) — this
traffic does not pass through, or get collected by, the Service Provider.

The Application maintains an internal debug log on your device for
troubleshooting. This log may include local network details, such as the IP
address of your own inverter or gateway on your home network. The debug log
is stored only on your device and is never transmitted automatically — it is
only shared if you deliberately choose to export or send it (for example,
when reporting a bug), and only with whoever you choose to send it to.

### Cross-platform licence sharing

GivLocal Pro and Installer purchases can optionally be shared between your
own devices without paying twice — see [Cross-Platform Sharing](/cross-platform-sharing/).
If you use this feature, the Application sends a randomly generated device
identifier and proof of your purchase (a signed transaction receipt from the
App Store, or a purchase token from Google Play) to GivLocal's licence
server, which verifies the purchase and issues or redeems a short code. No
personal information — such as your name, email address, or account details —
is collected or transmitted as part of this process, and this feature does
not need to be used at all if you'd rather not share data with the licence
server.

### Does the Application collect precise real-time location information?

No. The Application does not collect precise information about the location of
your device.

### Why does the Application request Location permission?

If you enable Automatic Switching (see [Automatic Wi-Fi Switching](/remote-access/automatic-switching/)),
the Application requests Location permission (on newer versions of Android, the
Nearby Wi-Fi Devices permission may be used instead). This is required by iOS
and Android to read the name of the Wi-Fi network your device is currently
connected to. It is used only to detect your current Wi-Fi network name, so
GivLocal can connect to your inverter directly when you're on a known home
network and via Tailscale otherwise. Precise location or GPS data is never
accessed, stored, or transmitted. If you do not use Automatic Switching, this
permission is never requested.

### Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the
Application?

Since the Application does not collect personal information through normal use,
no personal data is shared with third parties as part of the application's
operation.

### What are my opt-out rights?

Because the Application does not collect personal information during normal
use, uninstalling the app removes it from your device and effectively opts you
out of any application behaviour.

### Contact and voluntarily provided information

If you contact the Service Provider directly or voluntarily provide information
by other means, you may request deletion of that information by emailing
support@givlocal.app.

### Children

The Application is not intended for children under 16 years of age (or such
higher age as required by applicable law). The Service Provider does not
knowingly solicit or collect personal data from children using the Application.
If a child under 16 (or the applicable age) has voluntarily provided personal
information by other means, a parent or guardian must provide consent where
required by law.

### Security

Because the Application does not collect personal data through normal use, the
risk of personal data exposure is minimal. However, no security system is
completely secure. The Service Provider implements reasonable safeguards to
protect systems and any data it holds.

### Data Breach Notification

Since the Application does not collect personal data through normal use, the
risk of a data breach affecting your personal data is low. If a breach occurs
involving any data you have voluntarily provided, the Service Provider will
notify you as required by applicable law.

### Changes

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material
changes will be posted with an effective date. Where required by law, the
Service Provider will seek your consent to material changes before they take
effect. Previous versions are available on request by contacting the Service
Provider at support@givlocal.app.

**Effective date:** 01/06/2026

### Your Consent

If you voluntarily provide information to the Service Provider and processing is
based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting
processing carried out before withdrawal.

### Contact Us

If you have questions about privacy while using the Application, or about these
practices, contact the Service Provider at support@givlocal.app.
