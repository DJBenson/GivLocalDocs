INVLocal connects to your GivEnergy hardware directly over your home network.

## Before you start

Make sure:

- Your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your GivEnergy inverter or gateway
- The device is powered on and reachable on the network

<Aside type="tip">
  If you have a Gateway, connect to the Gateway rather than individual inverters - it provides a unified view of your whole system.
</Aside>

## Automatic discovery

Open INVLocal and tap **Devices**. The app will scan your local network and automatically find any compatible GivEnergy devices. Tap a device to connect to it.

See [Device Discovery](/getting-started/device-discovery/) for more detail.

## Reconnecting quickly

After a successful connection, INVLocal remembers the last working connection method and reuses it on later connects, so reconnecting after returning to the app is usually instant. If you mark a device as a favourite (available with **INVLocal Pro**), it can connect automatically on launch.

## Manual connection

If automatic discovery doesn't find your device, you can enter its IP address manually:

1. Find your inverter or gateway's IP address from your router's DHCP client list
2. Open INVLocal and tap **Devices**
3. Scroll to **Manual Connection**, enter the IP address, and tap **Connect**

<Aside type="tip">
  Assign a static IP (or DHCP reservation) to your inverter in your router settings so the address doesn't change.
</Aside>
