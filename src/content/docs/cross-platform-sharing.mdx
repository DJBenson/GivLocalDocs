If you've bought INVLocal Pro or Installer on one platform, you can unlock the same tier on your other device (iOS or Android) without paying again. There's no account or sign-in - a short code links the two devices directly.

The **License** section in Settings shows your current tier and the actions available for it:

## Setting up the device that made the purchase

1. Open **Settings → License**
2. Tap **Share Code** next to **Activate on Another Device**

<a href={licenseInstaller.src} target="_blank" rel="noopener">
  <Image src={licenseInstaller} alt="Settings License section showing an Installer tier with Activate on Another Device and a Share Code button" />
</a>

3. INVLocal verifies your purchase and generates a one-time code, shown as text and a QR code
4. Share it with your other device - use the **Share** button, copy the code, or let the other device scan the QR code

  <Image src={activateNotYet} alt="Activate on Another Device screen showing the QR code, share code, and Not yet activated status" />
</a>

## Setting up the device without a licence

1. Open **Settings → License**
2. Tap **Enter Code** next to **Purchased on Another Device?**
3. Type in the code - dashes are optional - or tap **Scan QR Code** and point at the other device's screen
4. Once accepted, Pro/Installer unlocks immediately on this device

## Restrictions

<Aside type="note">
  This is designed to let one owner use both of their own devices, not to share a single purchase between separate people.
</Aside>

- **One device at a time.** A code can only be activated on a single device. While it's active, the donor device can't hand out a new code to a different device without first deactivating the current one.
- **Occasional connectivity required.** The shared entitlement checks in with INVLocal's license server from time to time. It keeps working for a while if the device is offline, but if it can't check in for an extended period the shared unlock will lapse until it reconnects - everything else in INVLocal continues to work normally regardless of connectivity.

## Deactivating a licence

Use this to move your entitlement to a different device, or to replace one that's lost, broken, or being retired - deactivation works from either side.

- **From the device that received the code:** open **Settings → License** and tap **Deactivate** next to **Linked Device**. This immediately drops the shared entitlement on that device and frees the original device to share with a new one.
- **From the device that made the purchase:** open **Settings → License**, tap **Share Code**, then tap **Generate a new code instead**. Confirm the warning - this immediately invalidates the previous code and disconnects whichever device was using it, which is useful if that device is lost or broken and you can't deactivate it yourself.
